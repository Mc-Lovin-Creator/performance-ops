# Licences and what they mean for selling the product

| Component | Licence | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App code (`app/src`, `backend`, `frontend`, `tests`) | Yours (add a LICENSE before sharing) | Can be closed and sold. |
| Anatomy pack (`app/assets/anatomy-*.txt`) and the pipeline output | **CC BY-SA 4.0** (Z-Anatomy), built on BodyParts3D (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan) | Commercial use is allowed. The anatomy files themselves, and any edited versions of them, must stay under CC BY-SA with credit. The app that displays them does not become open source. |
| three.js | MIT | Free to use; keep the notice. |
| Booking flow ideas from Cal.com | MIT | Free to use; credited in the app. |

## Decision for investors

1. **Keep the anatomy open (recommended now).** Publish the anatomy pack (it is already credited in the app). The value is the evidence, the workflow and the data, not the mesh.
2. **License a commercial model later** (for example BioDigital, Zygote, Elsevier Complete Anatomy) if you need closed, higher-detail assets. Budget and terms vary; get quotes.

The app credits Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D on the anatomy page. Do not remove that credit.
